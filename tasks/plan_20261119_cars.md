## Off road: wheelspin and mud (user, 2026-11-20)

User: "when offroad wheels should spinn more easily throwing mud into air..."

- [ ] **Wheel slip in CarSim.** Off road, and less so on gravel, the driven wheels break traction under power more easily.
  - Wheel speed runs ahead of ground speed, the revs flare, and less of the power reaches the ground.
  - Each car behaves differently: the 911 and Volvo spin easily; the buggies and the Ranger (4WD, knobbly tyres) grip better but still spin.
  - The wheel meshes turn at wheel speed, not ground speed.
- [ ] **Mud and dirt thrown from the tyres** (CarEffects; the Dust and Spray slots are empty today).
  - Particles come off the driven wheels in proportion to slip × wheel speed, thrown up and back.
  - The colour follows the surface: brown mud and clods off road, grey-beige stones and dust on gravel, nothing (or a little tyre smoke) on asphalt.
  - A splash in water. The particles fall under gravity, and a few clods are bigger.
  - Optional: a dirt build-up on the car body.
- [ ] Budget: a fixed particle cap per wheel, nothing beyond about 90 m, and no measurable frame-time change.
- [ ] Tests: slip grows with power and falls with grip per surface and car, the emission rate follows slip, and nothing is emitted on asphalt at steady speed.

## Review (2026-11-20, car chooser + buggies + 50 km/h fix)

Done:
- [x] Eight cars on the start screen: Porsche 911, 911 Carrera 3.2, 911 Turbo (930), Volvo 240 Turbo, MV6 '83, Ford Ranger
      Offroad, Baja Bug, Buggy Type 736. Each has its own prefab from `Editor/CarGarageBuilder` and its own `CarSpec`.
      The builder scales each model to the real length, turns its nose to +Z, splits the wheels where the Carrera and
      the 736 have all four in one mesh, bakes the body into car space for the dents, adds the exhaust on the tailpipes
      and sets the body box from the model.

      sent to peers, and the ghosts use the right prefab ("buggy" from the JS game → Baja Bug).

- [x] Credits in the help screen, README, unity/README and Art/Cars/README. LICENSE.txt added for both buggies; the orphan
      zip metas were deleted.
- [x] Tests: GarageTests (EditMode), TopSpeedTests (EditMode), GarageSceneTests (PlayMode).
- [x] 50 km/h bug (coordinator, from the user):
      - Confirmed cause: OnRoad only knew the one-lane stage line. Fixed: any drawn road now counts as road.
      - A second limit was car.js's single air drag for every car, which held the 911 to ~175 km/h. Fixed with per-car `Aero` and `TopSpeed`.
